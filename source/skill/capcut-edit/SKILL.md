## 1. Purpose and scope

Own CapCut-specific editing and its editable timeline handoff. Keep the encoded-video project lane with `video-production`; it routes here for this capability. Default acceptance is correct main-speaker content and captions on **one visual line**, at most six displayed words per cue. Each cue stays entirely within one reviewed logical sentence; aim for five or six words only when its meaning permits, and allow a shorter remainder. Bind sentence identities and raw word ranges before display formatting, independently of transcription segments. Review the whole retained transcript for indivisible noun, adjective and verb phrases, proper names, terms and number-plus-unit expressions. Bind each reviewed protected span to contiguous raw word IDs inside one reviewed sentence before display conversion; no cue boundary may split it. Five-to-six-word packing yields to meaning, and shorter meaningful cues or leftovers are allowed, including number-unit or comparison units; never force them into a neighboring cue just to fill a count. Compatible nested or overlapping spans must fit together in one cue. A genuinely indivisible span or overlap union exceeding the active displayed-word ceiling is an evidenced conflict, never permission to abbreviate, drop words, split it silently or shrink text. A longer head-plus-attachment composition may be split only at explicitly reviewed constituent boundaries; it is not automatically an atomic protected span. Supplied review assertions do not prove grammar or heard audio. Include contextual auxiliary/modal verb units such as sẽ là, có thể, không thể or đã từng when reviewed as one unit; protect the full reviewed expression rather than banning a bare word or forcing every occurrence into the same grouping. Keep reviewed internal commas, remove any comma at a cue end and all full stops, and retain reviewed colons or question marks without inventing punctuation. Carry every previously user-requested or accepted protected span forward as a hard constraint in each regrouping; never remove or redefine it just to make a proposed boundary pass. Recheck all retained and new spans after every repair; only a newer explicit scoped user decision may supersede an accepted assertion, with its history and reason preserved. Lowercase ordinary words and retain only verified names in their proper case. For explicitly authorized, reviewed spoken lists, count the complete raw lead-in before display conversion: at most four words may use the reviewed short-lead-in replacement below. A longer lead-in containing an ordinal stays as the complete original sentence in one cue, including its spoken ordinal; only the following main-content cue receives the matching number and capitalized first content character. This long-lead-in-only exception may exceed the six-word ceiling, never the one-line readable/native proof requirement; it does not relax other cue limits. For a short lead-in, replace only the bound ordinal marker and reviewed lead-in with a numbered prefix such as `1.`; retain every consumed raw word ID in reversible transformation evidence. Keep the prefix with its first content word in one cue, capitalize only that word’s first character, then retain ordinary lowercase and verified-name case. This period after the reviewed ordinal is the only default added/full-stop exception; never infer a list, delete other words or change source audio. Validate complete list order and exact lead-in wording before conversion. Extend each caption end to the exact next caption start, preserving the first onset and last reviewed end. A future explicit user request may override these caption conventions in the task brief. Follow the content-and-captions checkpoint. Never replace it with word-by-word captions, two rows, or a flattened final deliverable. For final acceptance, add B-roll only after that checkpoint and within the user's scope. An explicitly authorized controlled review draft may combine prepared picture and content for review under the native-pipeline leaf; it must not label that preparation a passed final checkpoint. Record whether base acceptance is user-reported or personally verified; never convert user-reported acceptance into the agent's own hearing/playback claim. Default to one placement per verified original source across the whole current timeline, including unchanged overlays; another source window, alias, crop or track does not reset usage. A shortage of fresh relevant material holds the placement. Only an explicit current-task, snapshot-bound user override may select the leaf's conditional reuse mode. Recognize contextual meaning units on raw reviewed sentences before proposing cues: noun/name/number units, verb/modal/negation/aspect and beneficiary, adjective/degree/comparison, preposition/recipient and idiom.
