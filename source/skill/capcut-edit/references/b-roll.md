For an explicitly authorized separate controlled review draft, read [native pipeline](native-pipeline.md#controlled-review-draft). Prepared/model-only content, onsets or picture may be auditioned there with a complete pending inventory; they never become reviewed/heard/final PASS. All semantic, source, style and source-usage rules in this leaf remain in force. The detailed acceptance requirements below apply to the final checkpoint.

## Entry condition

B-roll stays within the user's explicit scope. Default `coverageMode: SPARSE` adds optional supporting footage; an explicitly bound voiceover-reading presentation may select `FULL_TIMELINE` to cover the entire picture. Start after the content-and-captions checkpoint passes. Bind actual proof and a frozen baseline for recovery; distinguish USER_REPORTED acceptance from personally verified source/native playback. A direct user report of checking speech/content and separate video/text editing plus save/reopen can be recorded with its exact task evidence, without claiming the agent heard or operated those checks. Keep every original acceptance criterion and final B-roll/native proof. A route comparison or style name alone does not authorize paid generation, external uploads, unrelated downloads or publication. Prefer permitted supplied footage. Generated visuals retain their actual format owner's separate gates.

## Meaning and sources

In SPARSE mode, aim for approximately one B-roll placement every five to ten seconds, with content relevance and added information taking priority. This is a soft planning target, not a hard quota or a reason to show an irrelevant shot. Review the entire placement plan and each clip/span. Record what the imagery explains or adds, exact source identity/rights, source in/out and evidence. Do not imply that generic footage proves a named site, distance, amenity or client/product fact. Default to one placement per verified original source identity in the whole current timeline. Another window, renamed file, crop, alias or new track is still the same source. Multiple excerpts require the explicit conditional-reuse override below; keep original source handles and meaningful purpose.

Each overlay lasts at least three seconds. Ten seconds is the normal soft upper target; a longer span needs a reviewed meaning reason. Choose the actual length needed, not ten seconds because a slot exists. Never pad, freeze, repeat or stretch footage just to fill ten seconds. Preserve the main speaker's video, continuous audio, content cuts, caption text/objects and timeline duration. Put silent B-roll on a separately editable video track; mute its source audio to avoid duplication. Use the selected style's crop/motion choices and source rotation, without obscuring captions or shrinking their readable floor.

## Full timeline voiceover-reading variant

Select `coverageMode: FULL_TIMELINE` only when the task explicitly requests full picture coverage and binds `sourcePresentationRole: VOICEOVER_READING` with reviewed coverage evidence and its truthful USER_REPORTED or AGENT_VERIFIED basis. Bind `coverageReview` with `reviewed`, `evidenceRef`, `evidenceBasis`, the exact `baseSnapshotSha256`, `durationTicks`, `firstCaptionId` and `lastCaptionId`; stale role/coverage evidence cannot validate a newly recut base. Do not infer this role from a seated person, transcript, filename or audio stream. Preserve the ordinary SPARSE mode for other work. Full coverage overrides empty cadence spaces, not semantic relevance, source rights, minimum three seconds, source-class 5/4 exclusions or native proof.

The B-roll union must be exactly [0, current timeline duration], contiguous without a gap or overlap. Interior transitions use actual unchanged caption boundaries. Permit only the first picture to begin at timeline zero before the first caption, and the last picture to finish at the timeline end after the last reviewed caption end; record these head/tail exceptions against the frozen base. Do not move the first speech caption earlier, extend the last caption, invent captions, or split main video to satisfy picture coverage. Keep a separate silent editable opaque picture layer above the main picture and below captions; do not alter the accepted voice track, audio gain, source chronology or cuts.

Choose meaning-led source windows from actually reviewed images/motion. Footage supplies professional context, not proof of a contract, payment, alleged project defect, named person's role or legal/financial assertion. Avoid identifiable brands or sites under unsupported negative claims. Prefer varied useful shots of approximately five to ten seconds, but preserve the three-second minimum and evidenced over-ten-second exceptions. Do not freeze, repeat, speed/stretch or fill with unrelated shots. If permitted meaningful imagery cannot cover the duration, report the specific missing visual role rather than fabricate evidence. After any authorized content recut, including qualifying head/tail removal, rebuild coverage on the new final edited-audio caption clock and actual new timeline bounds; move a boundary only to a current caption edge or merge/repartition neighboring accepted scenes when a span would fall below three seconds. Preserve reviewed source windows and style; record any authorized extension separately.

## Source classification and edge exclusions

For each source, bind `sourceOrigin` (`CAMERA`, `AI` or unresolved `UNKNOWN`), `voiceClassification` (`HAS_VOICE`, `NO_VOICE`, unresolved `UNKNOWN` or AI-only `NOT_APPLICABLE`), and reviewed classification evidence with its actual USER_REPORTED or AGENT_VERIFIED basis. If evidence or origin is missing/uncertain, or a CAMERA source has unknown voice, return to the existing [Gate0 interview](gate0.md) with one targeted question for those consequential facts before selecting/applying that source. A reviewed AI origin is already exempt from the camera edge rule: its voice may remain UNKNOWN or NOT_APPLICABLE without an extra voice question. This resolves factual brief data, not an extra B-roll approval. Do not infer origin from a camera-like image, filename or folder; do not infer voice from the existence/absence of an audio stream, mute flags, an empty transcript or ASR silence. A direct user classification stays user-reported, not the agent's own listening/source-origin proof. Generated visuals keep their owner's separate gates.

Only a source classified CAMERA plus NO_VOICE excludes the first five seconds and last four seconds on that source's clock. Every excerpt, including repeated excerpts from the same clip, must satisfy source-in >= 5 seconds and source-out <= actual source duration minus 4 seconds. This never removes time from the main timeline or changes caption anchors. Camera sources with voice and AI-generated sources have no mandated 5/4 exclusion; all still obey ordinary actual source bounds.

Use the actual probed source duration and evidence, not a rounded native material duration. Supply `actualDurationTicks` as conservative integer microseconds from that actual duration (floor sub-microsecond precision rather than round upward), plus `actualDurationEvidenceRef`. The helper does not substitute legacy `durationTicks` or a native rounded duration. The conditional source must be at least twelve seconds to leave the hard three-second usable excerpt after five plus four seconds are excluded. A shorter source cannot supply such an excerpt: choose another reviewed source or retain the main shot; do not pad, shorten the three-second floor, invent a class or silently waive exclusions.

## Source usage, fresh footage and conditional reuse

Inventory the whole current target timeline before planning, including unchanged B-roll in an incremental edit. Bind every actual source identity to its verified file SHA-256 and the frozen base snapshot. Different source IDs, renamed paths, aliases, crops or transforms do not reset known identity or source lineage. SHA-256 detects byte-identical aliases; it does not detect visual similarity or a cropped/transcoded copy with different bytes. For a known derivative, plan against the verified original identity and evidenced original-source window/offset, keeping a crop as a render transform. If that identity/clock cannot be represented by the current helper, hold that source rather than mark a new hash as fresh. When a supplied source is a known derivative, declare optional `sources[].derivedFromSourceId` naming its verified original supplied source ID. A declared different-hash derivative is HELD until the plan uses the verified original file and evidenced original source-window clock, with any crop as a native render transform; an unknown/unrepresentable original link is also held. An absent/null declaration never proves that an unknown source is fresh. Caller/native lineage review remains required; this update adds no derivative-recognition or derivative-clock mapping engine. The default `sourceReuseMode: NO_SOURCE_REPEAT` holds every second placement of the same original identity, including nonoverlapping or touching source windows. Only a newer explicit, snapshot-bound user request may select `REVIEWED_CONDITIONAL_REUSE` and permit multiple nonoverlapping excerpts. In the conditional mode only, overlapping reuse means any **positive overlap** of half-open source intervals `[in,out)` from the same identity; excerpts whose source edges merely touch do not overlap.

In default `NO_SOURCE_REPEAT`, choose a wholly fresh suitable original source; never reuse a seen source just because it has unused frames, enough time has passed or other material is exhausted. If suitable fresh imagery is insufficient, hold the affected placement and report the missing visual role; SPARSE may retain the accepted main picture, while FULL_TIMELINE remains incomplete. Do not silently authorize generation, fill or a source repeat.

Only `REVIEWED_CONDITIONAL_REUSE` preserves the previous fresh-source → unused-window → conditional-overlap preference. Bind `sourceReuseOverride` with `reviewed: true`, `authority: explicit-user-request`, `evidenceBasis: USER_REPORTED`, exact `mode`, current `baseSnapshotSha256`, nonempty `userQuote` and `evidenceRef`. An invented quote is not authority. Existing task approvals must actually cover this changed policy; a style name, continue request, material shortage or `exhausted: true` never supplies the override. Under that explicitly selected mode, prefer a wholly fresh suitable source, then an unused suitable window of an already-seen source. For an overlapping reuse, the new target START minus the **maximum prior target END** among every overlapping same-identity excerpt must be at least 180000000 microseconds. Exactly three minutes is allowed. Target start-to-start distance, source-recording time and waiting in real time do not satisfy this rule. Check the combined current/proposed timeline in chronological order, including a later unchanged placement affected by an earlier insertion; an ID change must not hide it.

For the explicitly authorized conditional mode, reaching three minutes is only the cooldown condition; it never overrides default no-source-repeat. Reuse additionally requires reviewed exhaustion of eligible unused footage for that placement's meaning. Review every supplied source: record its exhaustive suitable source windows, or an evidenced irrelevant/unavailable exclusion. Subtract the union of all previously used source intervals by verified identity from those windows before the current placement. Any remaining eligible contiguous window of at least three seconds defeats an exhaustion claim, even if the preferred slot is longer and needs caption-led repartitioning. Do not reserve unused footage for a future slot to hide it, omit an alias, or use an `exhausted: true` assertion as proof. Keep actual source bounds, conditional camera 5/4 exclusions, caption anchors, minimum three seconds and meaning-led duration unchanged.

An unknown classification, incomplete usage inventory, uncertain eligibility or unresolved exhaustion holds the affected placement and prevents a whole-timeline reuse PASS. Preserve unchanged baseline violations as reported/HELD, never retroactively certify them or silently repair an accepted project. A new placement may not introduce or hide a conflict with existing footage. If meaningful permitted material cannot cover a FULL_TIMELINE gap, name the missing visual role and material dependency; do not force unrelated imagery, a silent A-roll fallback, frozen/repeated filler, paid generation or a new upload.

### Provided-plan input and limits

Use the existing `--mode broll` and `capcut-broll-plan/v1` shape. Required `sourceUsageReview` contains `reviewed`, `evidenceRef`, truthful `evidenceBasis`, `scope: WHOLE_CURRENT_TIMELINE`, matching `baseSnapshotSha256` and `existingPlacements`. Each existing row binds `id`, `sourceId`, `sourceInTick`, `sourceOutTick`, `startTick` and `endTick`; optional `eligibleSourceReview` uses the placement schema below when existing reuse/preference needs review. Only exact same-ID/geometry records may be deduplicated. A changed same-ID geometry or a retime/repartition after a recut requires explicit snapshot-bound reconciliation. Source identity uses the existing `sources[].sha256`, a verified actual 64-hex digest, not a guessed display ID or another input field.

Optional `sourceUsageReview.reconciliations` rows bind nonempty `existingPlacementIds` and `plannedPlacementIds`, plus `reviewed`, `evidenceRef`, truthful `evidenceBasis` and `reason`. IDs must be known and unique/disjoint on each side across groups. A reviewed N:M group replaces only its named old rows in the proposed usage ledger; preserve the complete old inventory/history and report the old/proposed clocks and review. A missing/unknown group holds or rejects changed geometry; it never waives cooldown, eligibility or source bounds. Reconciliation is provided-plan preparation, not a native deletion operation or proof that old clocks already belong to a newly recut timeline. Bind the current base identity and explicitly revalidate the proposed map before native writing.

Each planned placement supplies `eligibleSourceReview` with `reviewed`, `evidenceRef`, `evidenceBasis`, `scope: ALL_SUPPLIED_SOURCES`, `exhaustive: true`, matching `baseSnapshotSha256` and `sources`. Cover every supplied `sourceId` exactly once. Each row has `status` (`ELIGIBLE`, `IRRELEVANT`, `UNAVAILABLE` or `UNKNOWN`), `evidenceRef`, `windows` and `reason`. An ELIGIBLE row lists all reviewed suitable `sourceInTick`/`sourceOutTick` windows inside actual usable bounds. Normalize their union by sorting and merging overlaps or touching edges before subtracting prior usage; do not require callers to pre-split them. Two touching 1.5-second windows form one three-second unused window, so fragmentation cannot manufacture exhaustion. Exclusion/UNKNOWN rows have empty windows and a specific reason; UNKNOWN remains HELD. Same-hash aliases require compatible actual duration/classification and the same canonical eligible status/window union, so an alias cannot conceal unused footage. Do not fabricate a usable duration or review just to make a plan pass.

The helper echoes the fixed `sourceReusePolicy` and generates `sourceUsageLedger` and `sourceUsageConflicts`, including canonical source identity, previous overlapping placement IDs, observed cooldown, eligible unused windows and per-placement reasons/status. These are output fields, not a new identity input. It checks only the provided inventory, review assertions and arithmetic. The helper verifies each supplied file against its declared `sources[].sha256`; whole-timeline inventory completeness, imagery/meaning, rights, eligible-window/exhaustion review authenticity and actual native usage still require caller/native evidence, so mechanical validation does not certify them. Recutting, retiming, replacing or adding footage invalidates the affected usage/cooldown/exhaustion proof and requires a fresh snapshot-bound review before new native acceptance.

## Caption anchors

In SPARSE mode, begin exactly at a reviewed caption onset on the native project clock. FULL_TIMELINE interior anchors use the same clock, with only the explicit first/last picture-edge exceptions above. When selecting a suitable onset, prefer a nearby existing A-roll cut; this preference never moves the overlay away from the caption onset. No universal nearest-cut tolerance is inferred. Never split, trim, shift or replace main video merely to create a cut/caption intersection. If meaning warrants an onset without a nearby cut, keep the main video and record the selection reason.

End exactly at the end of the last caption covered by that overlay, except the recorded final FULL_TIMELINE picture edge. Caption boundaries inside the overlay impose no extra cuts or constraints. The span may cover multiple full caption cues. Respect the three-second minimum, select appropriate last-caption anchors and explain a meaningful over-ten-second exception. Use integer native microsecond ticks without rounding or replacing the approved caption clock. Preserve source/edit clock distinctions and all existing caption holds. No uniform duration allocation or forced ten-second filling.

## Preparation helper

The owned `scripts/prepare_cut.py --mode broll` validates a `capcut-broll-plan/v1` task plan, either directly or as `bRollPlan` inside the cut-plan wrapper. `templates/cut-plan.json` shows the unfilled shape; it is not approval or native evidence. Supply explicit coverage mode and any voiceover-reading role/review, the frozen base snapshot path/hash/duration, real checkpoint state, existing caption cues/cuts, permitted footage identity, actual duration/probe evidence, source origin/voice and classification evidence, rights, and reviewed placements/cadence. Supply `baseCheckpointEvidenceRef` and `baseCheckpointEvidenceBasis` (`USER_REPORTED` or `AGENT_VERIFIED`); missing or pending base proof does not pass this gate.

The helper writes a new task-owned `broll-spec.json` and `broll-report.json` only. It requires evidenced known origin and, for CAMERA only, known voice (otherwise Gate0 dependency). AI voice UNKNOWN/NOT_APPLICABLE needs no extra voice interview. Every source still requires actual-duration evidence and exact actual source bounds. The helper applies five-second head/four-second tail exclusions only to CAMERA plus NO_VOICE for every excerpt, and enforces caption anchors with only FULL_TIMELINE outer picture-edge exceptions, contiguous full-duration union for that mode, nonoverlap, minimum three seconds, reasoned over-ten-second duration, silent editable overlay objects and no main-video/audio/caption operations. It reports nearest existing cut and observed start gaps, without enforcing a uniform cadence or intersection. Default no-source-repeat holds any second use, including nonoverlapping windows. Only the exact current-base explicit conditional override permits multiple excerpts; overlapping reuse then also requires the source-usage/cooldown/exhaustion review above. It validates supplied identities/assertions and arithmetic, not actual semantic relevance, source-origin/voice classification or duration/rights authenticity, prior checkpoint completion or CapCut rendering. Outputs are PREPARED_ONLY only when no slot is held; otherwise they are PARTIAL_PREPARED_REVIEW_REQUIRED with HELD overlays omitted and `applyEligible: false`, while supplied base evidence retains its actual basis and post-overlay native acceptance stays pending.

## Acceptance

Immediately before every native filesystem write, run the mandatory [pre-native B-roll audit](pre-native-apply.md#mandatory-b-roll-inventory) on exact proposed bytes. It derives all non-main video objects, recomputes the current hash-bound plan and checks complete base/proposed usage; an optional checklist or a prepared report alone cannot satisfy it. Default no-source-repeat covers the entire current timeline including unchanged footage.

Inspect every inserted span and joins in native playback. Rebuild the whole current source-usage ledger from actual native source windows, including unchanged clips and aliases; check interval overlap, END-to-START cooldown and reviewed unused-window exhaustion on this exact revision, followed by save/reopen readback. Unknown or pre-existing violations remain reported/HELD, not a whole-timeline reuse PASS. Confirm actual source-duration evidence, known source origin and applicable camera voice with truthful basis, and the conditional five/four-second source exclusions for every applicable excerpt; unknown origin or camera voice remains a Gate0 dependency, not inferred acceptance; known AI origin does not require a voice interview. Confirm original speech, source windows, content/captions/timeline remain unchanged; all SPARSE overlays and FULL_TIMELINE interior transitions use the bound caption anchors, only the first/last full-picture edges may extend to the timeline bounds, and full-picture union has no gap or overlap; internal caption boundaries are unconstrained; duration and meaning exceptions match the reviewed plan; footage adds information without unsupported site/amenity claims. Check silent separate editable B-roll, crop/rotation, captions' readable one-line fit and no gaps, audio duplication or clipping. Save/close/reopen and read persistence. Recheck affected proof and nearby joins. Deliver base checkpoint and B-roll version/state, source list and actual remaining gaps. Base acceptance never substitutes for post-overlay native acceptance. Failed B-roll does not invalidate unchanged base proof; return to the parent outcome automatically.
